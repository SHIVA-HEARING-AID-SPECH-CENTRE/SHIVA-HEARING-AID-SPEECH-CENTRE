Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a hearing test before buying a hearing aid?

Yes. A proper hearing assessment helps determine the type and degree of hearing loss and supports an appropriate recommendation. Sudden hearing loss needs urgent medical assessment.

Can I ask about prices before visiting?

Yes. Call or message us with your preferred budget and device needs. Current models, prices and stock should be confirmed directly with the centre.

Are repairs and adjustments available for every brand?

Serviceability depends on the brand, model, fault and availability of parts. Contact us with the device details before visiting.

Is speech therapy available at the centre?

Please contact the centre to confirm current speech therapy availability, schedule and appointment options before visiting.
